How it works

Four steps to safety

From snapshot to spoken instructions — fast enough to use at the pharmacy counter.

01
📷

Scan the label

Upload a photo, use your camera, or pick a demo preset. Our Vision AI reads brand, dosage, batch and expiry.

02
🔍

Registry check

We cross-reference the product against the Rwanda FDA registry and flag counterfeit packaging patterns.

03
🧠

AI simplifies dosage

Dense pharmaceutical instructions are converted into plain Kinyarwanda and English — no jargon.

04
🔊

Voice playback

Audio in Kinyarwanda for elderly, non-literate and visually-impaired users — safety read out loud.
